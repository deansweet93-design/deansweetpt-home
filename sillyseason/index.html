01 · The drinks guide

You don't have to go dry. Just drink smarter.

I'm not going to tell you to skip every drink in December. I won't be. But knowing what's actually in your glass makes it way easier to make a good call on the night.

How I'd handle a big night

Eat a proper meal first

Get some protein in before you head out. Rocking up starving is how the 1am kebab happens.

Pick your drink before you get there

Spirit and soda, a light beer or a seltzer. Decide on the way, not at the bar.

Water between every drink

Slows you down more than anything else will. Your head will thank you tomorrow too.

Set your number early

Decide how many you're having before the first one. Deciding after the fourth never works.

Don't skip meals to save up

Banking calories all day for drinks just gets you drunk quicker, and you'll eat more later anyway.

Next day, back to normal

A walk, a decent brekkie with protein and plenty of water. No punishment cardio needed.

02 · The bare minimum

Results doing the bare minimum

December isn't the month to chase a PB or a big cut. The goal is to hold onto what you've built so January isn't a restart. Good news is keeping your results takes way less work than building them.

Train

Twice a week

Two 30 minute full body sessions is enough to hold onto your strength and muscle.

Eat

Protein every meal

A palm sized serve at each meal. If you only keep one food habit this month, make it this one.

Move

7 to 8k steps

Beach walks, walking to the shops, a lap of the block after Christmas lunch. It all counts.

Mindset

Maintain, don't chase

Holding your weight through December is a win. Seriously.

Twice a week. 3 rounds. Rest about a minute between sets and leave 1 to 2 reps in the tank.

03 · Planning around events

Plan the big days so they don't run the whole month

Work drinks, the kids' break up, a few BBQs, Christmas, Boxing Day, New Year's. When it's all just "December" it turns into a free for all. Map it out and it gets heaps easier.

Write them all down

Every event from now until New Year's. Straight into your calendar so you can see what you're dealing with.

Pick your big ones

Christmas Day? Enjoy it, that's what it's for. The Tuesday arvo work drinks probably don't need to be a big one. Pick a few to go all in on and keep the rest chill.

Lock your training in first

Put your two sessions in the calendar, then fit the events around them. A morning session on event days is a good shout.

Don't starve yourself on the day

Normal brekkie and lunch with protein. Turning up hungry never ends well.

Protein first at the event

Prawns, ham, chicken skewers first, then go for the food you actually love. Skip the stuff you don't care about. Fill a plate once and go chat away from the food table.

Next day, back to normal

No skipping meals, no two hour cardio sessions. Just your usual day, and keep moving.
