What this actually is

A structured eight-week block of training and nutrition coaching, run as a group with a fixed start and end date.

You get a program built for three sessions a week, a protein and calorie target that fits how you actually eat, and a check-in every Sunday that I read and reply to. Same programming whether you train with me in person or from your own gym.

Three is the minimum that works, not a cap. If you'd rather train four or five days, say so and I'll write the extra days in.

It finishes on 29 November — a week before summer, and well before December turns into a month of parties and write-offs. That's deliberate.

One thing it isn't: a detox, a shred, or a plan built around removing food groups. If that's what you're after, this will disappoint you.

Sunday check-ins

Five things, every Sunday night. Takes about two minutes.

  1. Sessions completed this week
  2. Days you hit the habit we're working on
  3. Bodyweight, if you're tracking it
  4. One photo — same spot, same light
  5. What got in the way this week

Every check-in gets a reply from me. Not an automated one.

Track your weight if you want to, but know what you're looking at. It swings a couple of kilos on salt, sleep, a big meal or where you are in your cycle, and reading Tuesday against Monday tells you nothing. Eight weeks of it tells you plenty — and watching that line drop while the number bounces around underneath is one of the more useful things you'll learn in this.

Photos are yours. Take them every week because the eight-week comparison is worth more than any number, but you don't have to send them to me, and I'll never use one for anything without asking you first.

Questions

I haven't trained in years. Is this too advanced?

No. The program has a regression for every movement, and week two exists specifically to get technique right before anything gets heavy. Most people in this are coming back after a long break.

What if I miss a week?

You'll miss a week. Life happens, and the plan assumes it. Missing a week doesn't end your challenge — going quiet for three does, which is what the Sunday check-in is there to catch early.

Do I need a gym for the online version?

Yes, or a home setup with a barbell or dumbbells and something to pull on. Tell me what you've got access to and the program gets built around it.

Do I have to pay it all up front?

No. You can pay weekly across the eight weeks instead — $75 a week in person, $40 online. Same everything, it just spreads the cost. Say which you'd prefer when you sign up.

I like training four or five days a week. Is three all I get?

Three is the floor, not the ceiling. Tell me and I'll program the extra days properly, rather than you making them up and undoing the ones that count.

What happens on 29 November?

You get your final numbers and a plan for keeping them. Plenty of people carry on into ongoing coaching, but there's no auto-renewal and nothing rolls over unless you say so.

Can I do this with a mate?

Yes, and you should — the ones who bring someone finish at a much higher rate. Put their name in the reply to your waitlist email and I'll put you in the same session slot. To be clear though, you each pay your own way. Two people, two spots.
